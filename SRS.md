# Software Requirements Summary

The prototype provides synthetic equipment, fault, knowledge and analytics workflows for Operations, Systems Automation, maintenance sections, supervisors and management. It does not claim to support, connect to or control real KPC operational infrastructure.

Current functional scope includes:

- Tiered roles and section membership, section-scoped equipment and fault access, and role-based user management.
- Fault reporting and triage from Operations through Systems Automation to a maintenance section, with repeat-fault context.
- Per-fault investigation history with multiple possible diagnoses, attributed diagnostic evidence, and successful, failed or in-progress interventions.
- Technician/engineer work-procedure contributions after a successful intervention and restoration.
- Section Chief Technician/Chief Engineer technical review, clarification requests and contributor revisions; Supervisor publication after technical approval.
- Versioned approved knowledge, colleague-attributed reuse and post-restoration outcome confirmation.
- HR-only, date-filtered contribution evidence and response/restoration timing measures that do not disclose individual fault narratives. Report volume does not earn recognition points; any point weighting is explicitly illustrative.
- General Manager O&M section handoff, assignment, repeat-routing, knowledge reuse and response-time baselines; no claim of downtime savings without a measured baseline.
- Executive-summary-only CEO analytics, offline event queuing and synthetic demonstration data.
- Repeat-fault reporting flags 90-day recurrence and routes directly to the equipment-owning standby section.
- Troubleshooting ranks possible causes with quick checks, confirmed fixes, recurrence warnings and risk/supervisor governance.
- Operational dashboards show median minutes for each critical handoff and a repeat-versus-first-fault comparison against a 60% improvement target, while explicitly preserving baseline limitations.
- Contributor recognition follows a verified-contribution model and is ready for SOCA integration without depending on report volume.
- SAP integration is deferred until after operational stabilisation; only read-only equipment, location and permit/reference integration will be considered, with write-back disabled.

The prototype is not a production appraisal policy and does not connect to real KPC operational infrastructure.
