## Guided sequence

1. Sign in as `operations` and report a fault.
2. Sign in as `automation` and assign it to the appropriate section.
3. Sign in as `electrical` and acknowledge the fault.
4. Review seeded recurring faults and approved knowledge.
5. Sign in as `supervisor` to inspect approval workflows.
6. Sign in as `management` to review database-derived synthetic analytics.
7. Disconnect the network and submit another fault; it is queued locally.
8. Restore connectivity and press **Sync now**.
